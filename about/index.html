About this site

Updated 10 October 2026

CommercialEVs.co.uk publishes plain, sourced guides to commercial electric vehicles in the UK: the rules, grants, taxes and running costs that matter when a business is choosing between diesel and electric vans.

How we check facts

  • Every figure links to its source, listed at the end of each page.
  • We use official sources where we can: GOV.UK, TfL, Ofgem, Transport Scotland and SMMT statistics. Where we cite a trade publication, it's because the official page wasn't available or the publication explains the official figure.
  • Each page shows the date it was last updated. Prices, grant rates and rules change, so always check the source before making a decision.
  • Where a figure depends on assumptions, such as a van's fuel economy in a cost comparison, we state the assumption and how to use your own numbers.
  • We don't copy text or images from other sites. The charts and images on this site are our own work, and the guides link to the sources of their figures instead of reproducing them.
  • Figures from GOV.UK contain public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Corrections

If you spot something wrong or out of date, tell us through the contact form and say which page and figure it's about. We fix confirmed errors and update the page date.
