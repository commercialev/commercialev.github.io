FAQ

Common questions

Can I buy just one of the domains?

Yes. Each domain can be bought on its own, but we'd encourage buying both together so you own the whole category and keep competitors out.

What's the asking price?

We're open to sensible offers. Send yours through the enquiry form and we'll get back to you, usually within one working day.

How does the transfer work for a .co.uk domain?

.co.uk domains are managed by Nominet. Once payment is secured in escrow, we change the domain's registrar tag (IPS tag) to your registrar. You then accept it in your own account. It usually takes less than a day.

Is the purchase safe?

Yes. We use a reputable escrow service, so your money is only released to us once you have full control of the domain.

Do I need to be based in the UK?

No. Anyone can own a .co.uk domain, although it's naturally best suited to businesses serving UK customers.
