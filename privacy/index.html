Privacy

Updated 3 October 2026

This site is run by the owner of the domain names CommercialEV.co.uk and CommercialEVs.co.uk. This page explains what data the site collects and why.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used. Google Analytics sets cookies and records things like the pages you view, your approximate location, and your device and browser. It isn't used to identify you.

Google processes this data on the site's behalf. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

To opt out, block cookies in your browser or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Offer form

The "Make an offer" button on the domain for sale page opens a Google Form. The details you enter are stored in Google Forms and used only to reply to your enquiry about buying the domains. They aren't sold or shared with anyone else.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete data held about you. To do this, use the enquiry form and say your message is about privacy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
